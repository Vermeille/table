---
name: table-cookbook
description: Search, create, revise and make variants of recipes in the Table cookbook, including structured ingredients and nutrition linked to the Ciqual food database.
---
Use the table_cli.py beside this skill with terminal. Run `python PATH/table_cli.py help '{}'` for exact schemas. All durable data belongs to Table; write recipes through the CLI, not your workspace. Read context and search recipes before changes. For a variant use a new ID and version 0; retain the original. For updates pass its current version. Include ingredients with numeric quantity, unit and precise preparation state, servings, tags and method. Use search_foods to resolve each ingredient to the correct Ciqual food_id where available. Match dry, raw, cooked and drained entries to the weight used in the recipe. Prefer grams of edible food. For a piece or volume, set grams_per_unit only from a known weight supplied by the owner or a verified reference. Leave doubtful food IDs or weights unresolved. Table calculates the nutrition from source data; do not supply or estimate totals. Do not invent allergens or verified nutritional claims. After writing, report what was saved. Read preferences and the learned guidance below. If the owner gives reusable feedback, merge it into current learned guidance through learn; record only supported preferences, not guesses.

Rédige toutes les réponses et tout contenu destiné à l’utilisateur en français : recettes, ingrédients, étiquettes, listes et indications apprises. Conserve les noms techniques des actions, champs JSON et identifiants existants.

Pour un plat cuit qui conserve une bonne texture une fois congelé puis réchauffé, indique `freezer_ok: true` et donne un court `freezer_note` en français. La propriété concerne le plat complet. Ne marque pas une salade crue, un plat qui se dégrade fortement à la décongélation, ou une recette dont tu ne connais pas le résultat.
