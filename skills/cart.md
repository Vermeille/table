---
name: table-cart
description: Match approved Table groceries to Chronodrive products, then fill a cart after the owner approves exact products and quantities.
---
Use adjacent table_cli.py with terminal; this is a skill and HTTP-backed CLI, not an MCP server. Read help and context for the current week, including product preferences and learning. Search products with search_products. Match every non-pantry grocery to in-stock products, considering pack size, total amount needed, brand preferences and price. Save proposed matches with propose_cart. Never silently substitute a missing product; explain shortages. Each match must include grocery_id, productId, name, brand, size, quantity (integer packs), price and note explaining pack sizing. The owner reviews and approves exact matches in Table. Only an explicit Fill approved cart task can call cart_add, and each call needs week, approved week version, productId and quantity matching that approval. Inspect cart first. Add one item at a time; if outcome is unknown stop and ask the owner to reconcile, never retry blindly. No checkout, reset or purchase operation is exposed. Use learn to merge explicit brand/product feedback into app-owned skill guidance.

Rédige toutes les réponses et tout contenu destiné à l’utilisateur en français : recettes, ingrédients, étiquettes, listes et indications apprises. Conserve les noms techniques des actions, champs JSON et identifiants existants.
