---
name: table-cart
description: Match approved Table groceries to Chronodrive products, then fill a cart after the owner approves exact products and quantities.
---
Use adjacent table_cli.py with terminal; this is a skill and HTTP-backed CLI, not an MCP server. Read help and context for the current week, including product preferences and learning. Search products with search_products. Match every non-pantry grocery to in-stock products, considering pack size, total amount needed, brand preferences and price. Save proposed matches with propose_cart. Never silently substitute a missing product; explain shortages. Each match must include grocery_id, productId, name, brand, size, quantity (integer packs), price and note explaining pack sizing. The owner reviews and approves exact matches in Table. After the owner approves exact products, Table’s Fill approved cart button starts a durable app-managed fill job. Use fill_status with the week to read its authoritative progress. Do not issue per-item cart_add calls, do not spawn background CLI loops and do not claim the cart is complete while the job is pending. The app executes and verifies each approved quantity, independently of this chat turn. If a product is rejected or uncertain, report the job’s exact per-item status and direct the owner to its resume/review controls. No checkout, reset or purchase operation is exposed. Use learn to merge explicit brand/product feedback into app-owned skill guidance.

Rédige toutes les réponses et tout contenu destiné à l’utilisateur en français : recettes, ingrédients, étiquettes, listes et indications apprises. Conserve les noms techniques des actions, champs JSON et identifiants existants.

La sélection peut contenir des exclusions décidées par le propriétaire (`cart.excluded`) : articles déjà en réserve ou non achetés cette fois. Respecte ces décisions et ne les réintroduis pas dans tes propositions. Les articles sans correspondance apparaissent dans l’application avec des actions de résolution explicites ; seul le propriétaire peut les exclure. Les recherches renvoient maintenant `image_url` pour afficher les photos des produits.
